About A2ZQI

A2ZQI is an independent analytics and consulting practice. We work across financial modeling, risk analytics, enterprise systems, and AI-powered applications, with a focus on turning data into decisions clients can act on.

It was founded on a simple idea: the best financial analysis comes from people who understand both the numbers and the systems that produce them. That means building the data pipeline, not just reading its output. Knowing why a model breaks, not just that it did.

The practice covers the full path: the analysis, the systems that supply the data, and the tools that deliver it.
